life note

A quiet daily log app for capturing small moments in a few lines.

life note is a minimal daily logging app.

Write down small moments, todos, habits, and everyday notes without turning your life into a project management system.

It is designed to feel light:

  • quick input
  • simple timeline
  • daily notes
  • lightweight search
  • optional habit and trend views
  • no unnecessary noise
