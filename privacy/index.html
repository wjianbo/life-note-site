Life Note Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-03

Life Note respects user privacy.

Notes are stored locally on the user's device.

If the user enables GitHub sync, notes may be uploaded to the GitHub account or repository configured by the user.

Life Note does not operate its own server to collect or store notes.

Life Note does not sell personal data.

If the user contacts support by email, the email address and message content are used only to respond to the inquiry.

Users can delete their local data by deleting app data or uninstalling the app.

For questions, contact support@listout.app.
